New Year trip comparison: 24 Dec to 2 Jan

Two-stop routes in warm places close to your ideal of about 30°C by day and 23°C at night, with the days split to get the most out of each stop. Open the arrow on any row for the recommended hotels and a day-by-day plan; tap any green or pink activity button to open its videos and photos. Use Demote to move a destination to the bottom section, and Restore to bring it back.

Most energy and shopping: Thailand

Krabi islands and Phi Phi, then Bangkok for shopping and the river fireworks on New Year’s Eve.

Something different: Vietnam

Sand dunes and kitesurfing in Mui Ne, then the buzz of Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta.

Easiest and great shopping: Malaysia

Visa-free. Langkawi’s cable car and islands, then Kuala Lumpur malls and the Petronas fireworks.

Most beautiful adventure: Palawan

Lagoons and shipwrecks in El Nido and Coron. Needs an embassy visa, so apply early.
